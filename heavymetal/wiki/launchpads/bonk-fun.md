# bonk.fun / Raydium LaunchLab

- **LaunchLab** is Raydium's bonding-curve launch program. **bonk.fun** (LetsBonk) is the most used front end on it,
  launched in 2025 by the BONK community; mints often end in `bonk`.
- Curve completes -> liquidity migrates to a Raydium pool (CPMM/AMM). Part of the platform fees has been routed to
  BONK ecosystem buybacks, which made it a narrative trade itself in mid-2025.
- heavymetal can trade and launch here through PumpPortal (`pool: bonk` / `launchlab`).

## Patterns
- Launchpad wars: when bonk.fun overtook pump.fun on daily launches in July 2025, copycat and bundled launches
  spiked on both. Volume share between launchpads moves fast; Trenches shows whether the tape is actually growing
  or just moving between venues.
- Same rug archetypes as pump.fun: [[rugs/dev-dump]], [[rugs/bundled-supply]].
