# Moonshot

- Bonding-curve launchpad associated with DEX Screener, known for fiat onboarding (card / Apple Pay) and for listing
  large trending tokens in early 2025.
- Tokens migrate to an AMM after the curve completes (Raydium, later Meteora).
- Pair URLs on DEX Screener carry `moonshot` labels; heavymetal reads them as ordinary pools.

Same archetypes as other launchpads: the program handles the contract, holders are the risk.
See [[launchpads/overview]] and [[rugs/overview]].
