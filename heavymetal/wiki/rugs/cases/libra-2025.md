# $LIBRA (February 14, 2025)

**What happened (as reported)**
- Argentina's president Javier Milei posted about $LIBRA, presented as a project to fund Argentine businesses.
- The token launched on Solana with **single-sided liquidity on Meteora** pools. Market cap rose to the billions
  within hours.
- Wallets linked to insiders withdrew the USDC/SOL side as buyers came in; on-chain analysts estimated on the order
  of $100M extracted. The price fell more than 90% the same day and Milei deleted the post.
- Investigations, lawsuits and resignations followed (including at Meteora); the launch team was linked to
  earlier tokens with the same pattern.

**Pattern**: [[rugs/liquidity-pull]] via single-sided liquidity ([[launchpads/meteora-dbc]]), plus
[[rugs/bundled-supply]]-style insider allocation. A famous promoter adds attention, not safety.

**Checks that would have flagged it**: liquidity owned by the deployer (not burned), supply concentrated in a few
wallets, a brand-new pool with a multi-billion FDV and thin real liquidity on the quote side.
