# $HAWK (December 4, 2024)

**What happened (as reported)**
- Influencer Haliey Welch ("Hawk Tuah") promoted the $HAWK token on Solana.
- It ran to a market cap of several hundred million dollars shortly after launch, then fell over 90% within
  hours.
- On-chain analysts (e.g. Bubblemaps) reported that a large share of supply had been captured by snipers and
  connected wallets at launch, and those wallets sold into the hype. The team denied insider selling; lawsuits
  followed.

**Pattern**: [[rugs/bundled-supply]] / sniper concentration on a celebrity launch, then a coordinated
[[rugs/dev-dump]]-style exit.

**Checks that would have flagged it**: top-10 concentration far above 40% excluding the pool, fresh wallets with
identical balances, a cluster on Bubblemaps, and FDV out of proportion to real liquidity.
