# Active mint authority

If mint authority is not revoked, the holder can mint unlimited new tokens and dump them into the pool.

- Launchpad tokens revoke it at creation.
- heavymetal counts "mint authority revoked" as a positive; an active one keeps a token out of HIGH CONVICTION.
- Some legitimate projects keep it for emissions; for a memecoin there is no good reason.
