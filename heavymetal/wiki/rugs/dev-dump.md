# Dev dump

**Mechanism**: the creator buys a big share at the bottom of the curve (in the create tx or via side wallets), waits
for graduation or a hype spike, and sells. A **slow rug** sells in pieces over days to keep the chart from showing
one red candle.

**Tells**
- creator wallet (RugCheck `creator`) still holding a large %, or funded side wallets holding it
- creator history: `scan_wallet` on the creator often shows a string of launches and sells
- big sells at round multiples of the entry

**Defence**: check the creator; avoid tokens where one non-pool wallet holds > 10%; size small on fresh graduations.
