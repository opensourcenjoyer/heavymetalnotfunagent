# Farmed volume

High volume looks like demand, but bots can fake it:

- **Wash loops**: the same wallets buy and sell to each other. Volume can be hundreds of times the liquidity.
- **Fee farming**: bots swap constantly through a near-zero-fee pool to farm volume points or rewards.
- **Volume bots**: services paid to make a chart look active.

Signs: volume many times larger than liquidity, few unique wallets doing huge numbers of swaps, a flat price
despite "huge" volume. Wizzy calls these tokens **hexed**.

```quiz
Q: A token traded 400 times its liquidity in a day with a flat price. Most likely?
- [x] Bots trading with themselves (wash volume)
- [ ] Massive real demand
- [ ] A liquidity lock

Q: Why does fake volume matter?
- [ ] It doesn't
- [x] It makes a token look popular when real buyers are few
- [ ] It burns the LP
```
