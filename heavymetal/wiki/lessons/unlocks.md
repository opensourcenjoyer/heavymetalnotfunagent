# Token unlocks

Many project tokens (not most memecoins) give team members, investors and treasuries tokens that are
**locked** and released over time on a **vesting** schedule. Each release is an **unlock**.

- An unlock adds tokens that can be sold. Big unlocks can bring selling pressure.
- A "cliff" releases a large amount at once; "linear" vesting drips it out.
- Compare the unlock size to daily trading volume: an unlock worth more than a few days of volume is large.

Wizzy cannot see unlock schedules yet, so it says the mist hides them rather than guessing.
