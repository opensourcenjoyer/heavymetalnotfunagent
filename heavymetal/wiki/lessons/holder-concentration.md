# Holder concentration

The **top 10 holders** share tells you how much of the supply a few wallets control.

- Under ~30% is usually healthy for a memecoin.
- Above ~50% means a handful of wallets can crash the price whenever they sell.
- Watch out: the largest "holder" is often the liquidity pool or a bonding curve, which is not a person.
- **Bundles** are many wallets funded by the same source that bought at launch. They look like many holders
  but act as one.

```quiz
Q: The top 10 wallets hold 70% of the supply. What is the risk?
- [x] A few wallets can crash the price by selling
- [ ] There is no risk; more holders is better
- [ ] The freeze authority is active

Q: The largest holder is the liquidity pool. Should that count as a whale?
- [ ] Yes, it can dump at any time
- [x] No, it is the trading pool, not a person
- [ ] Only if the LP is burned
```
