# Market cap vs FDV

- **Market cap** = price × circulating supply (tokens that can trade today).
- **FDV** (fully diluted valuation) = price × total supply (including tokens still locked or not yet minted).

When FDV is much larger than market cap, many tokens are still waiting to enter the market. As they unlock,
new sellers appear. For most fresh memecoins the two numbers are almost the same because the whole supply is
already out.

```quiz
Q: What is FDV?
- [x] Price multiplied by the total supply, including locked tokens
- [ ] Price multiplied by the tokens traded today
- [ ] The dollar value of the liquidity pool

Q: FDV is 10 times the market cap. Why might that matter?
- [x] Many more tokens may unlock later and add selling pressure
- [ ] It means the token is undervalued
- [ ] It means the liquidity is locked
```
