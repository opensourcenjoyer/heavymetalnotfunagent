# Liquidity

A token trades in a **pool**: a pile of the token on one side and SOL or USDC on the other. **Liquidity** is
the dollar value sitting in that pool.

- Deep liquidity (hundreds of thousands of dollars or more) lets people buy and sell without moving the price much.
- Thin liquidity means even a small sell can crash the price, and a big holder can drain the pool.
- Liquidity is not market cap. A token can show a $10M market cap with only $20K of liquidity behind it.
  That market cap cannot actually be cashed out.

Ask: if the top holder sold, how much of the pool would they take?
